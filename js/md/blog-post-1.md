## Why Business Plans Matter

In Borana, where businesses face unique challenges—from climate variability to infrastructure gaps—a well-prepared business plan is not just a document. It is the foundation of success. Banks, investors, and development partners require clear, evidence-based plans before committing resources.

## 5 Practical Steps to Create a Bankable Business Plan

### 1. Understand Your Market
Conduct thorough market research. Who are your customers? Who are your competitors? What are the pricing trends? In Borana, this often requires primary research through community consultations and local trader interviews.

### 2. Define Your Business Model
Use tools like the Business Model Canvas to clearly articulate your value proposition, customer segments, and revenue streams. Be specific about how your business will make money.

### 3. Develop Realistic Financial Projections
Include 3–5 year projections for income statements, cash flow, and balance sheets. Be conservative in your revenue estimates and comprehensive in your cost analysis. Account for seasonal income fluctuations common in Borana.

### 4. Identify and Mitigate Risks
What could go wrong? Drought, market price fluctuations, security issues along trade corridors, policy changes—identify these risks and develop clear mitigation strategies. Investors want to see that you have thought ahead.

### 5. Present a Clear Investment Roadmap
Show how much capital is needed, how it will be used, and what returns are expected. Make it easy for financiers to say "yes."
