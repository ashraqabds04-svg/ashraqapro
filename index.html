About Ashraqa BDS

Ashraqa Business Development Services (BDS) is a multi-disciplinary consulting firm based in Yabelo, South Oromia, Ethiopia. The name "Ashraqa" means "sunrise," reflecting our mission to bring new light, opportunity, and resilience to the businesses and communities of the region.

We provide end-to-end support—from business planning and financial management to market linkages and digital innovation—helping enterprises transform into sustainable, growing ventures.
